Visitors

Play the championship links where the Welsh Amateur has been played nine times. Visitors are always welcome, whether it’s a game with friends or a day out for your club or company. Pick a date to see your green fee, then book your tee time online.

You can use the practice facilities and the clubhouse too. Juniors under 18 pay half the green fee. To book by phone, call the office on 01745 854320, option 1.

County Card
Male full playing members of clubs in the Union of Flintshire Golf Clubs (UFGC) play for £25 a round, Monday to Thursday, from 1 April to 30 September. Book 48 hours ahead and bring proof of your club. Not at weekends, on bank holidays, on competition days or as part of a society. UFGC clubs
Courtesy golf
Visiting club captains in their year of office, and members of the PGA, are welcome to one courtesy round a year. Ask for it when you book, and bring proof on the day.
Reciprocal golf
The club does not offer reciprocal golf arrangements.
Buggies and trolleys
Twelve electric buggies at £30 a round. Please book ahead on 01745 854320, option 2. Electric and pull trolleys are also available to hire.
