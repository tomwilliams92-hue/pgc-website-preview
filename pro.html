Andrew Carr, PGA Professional

More than 30 years in golf, and Prestatyn’s club professional since May 2021.

Andrew started as a junior at Shrewsbury Golf Club, turned professional at 18 and became one of the youngest club professionals in the country at Rhuddlan in 1996. In his words, “It is an honour to be Club Professional at such a prestigious club and work at one of the best championship links in Wales.”

Call the pro shop on 01745 854320 or email andrewcarrpga@aol.com.

The pro shop

Newly refurbished, with equipment, clothing and shoes for ladies and men. A member of Foremost, Europe’s largest golf buying group, with a price match promise.

Coaching

Seven days a week, for beginners to professionals, using the practice ground, video and ball-flight analysis. One-to-one and group lessons all year.

Custom fitting

Ping, Titleist and Cobra, fitted on Trackman for loft, lie, length and shaft. Repairs and regripping, mostly while you wait.
