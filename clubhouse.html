A welcome after your round

Set against the sea and the course, the clubhouse has wonderful views, and everyone is welcome.

It was completely refurbished in 2020, with a separate spike bar, a dining room and a lounge, all served by a new double bar. On warm days, sit out on the balcony over the course.
