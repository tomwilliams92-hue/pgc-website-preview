Join Prestatyn Golf Club

A vibrant, forward-thinking members’ club on a championship links, with a friendly, relaxed atmosphere. Members stay: retention is exceptionally high.

  • Weekly competitions for every member, plus weekend competitions and knock-outs through the season
  • Friendly home and away matches against other local clubs
  • Bring up to three guests at a time at a reduced green fee
  • A busy social calendar, run by the members’ own sections
  • Coaching seven days a week with the club professional
