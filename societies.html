Society days

Prestatyn is a favourite with golf societies: a championship links, a warm welcome and the clubhouse to finish the day.

Every package includes full use of the course and the clubhouse. Food is locally sourced and homemade, from a bacon roll to a three-course meal. See your price straight away and request your date online.

What food can we have?
Coffee and a bacon roll, a full Welsh breakfast, lunch, or a one, two or three-course meal, all made in the clubhouse. Tell us about dietary needs on the form.
Can we hire buggies?
Yes. The club has twelve electric buggies at £30 a round; book them with your society.
Can we use the practice ground?
Yes. Societies have full use of the course, the practice facilities and the clubhouse.
