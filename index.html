Founded in 1905, Prestatyn is the most northerly course in mainland Wales: a championship links between the beach and the town, ranked fifth in North Wales by NCG and nine times host of the Welsh Amateur. Visitors, societies and new members are always welcome, and the clubhouse, refurbished in 2020, looks out over the course.
