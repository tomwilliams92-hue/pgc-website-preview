Celebrate at the club

The clubhouse is a lovely place in Prestatyn to celebrate: modern, comfortable and looking out over the course.

Our team will help you plan your event down to the smallest detail: room layout, table decoration, entertainment, menus and drinks. On warm days guests can sit out on the balcony, overlooking a large part of the course. Menus are hot, cold or made to order, and we will cater for your guests’ preferences.
